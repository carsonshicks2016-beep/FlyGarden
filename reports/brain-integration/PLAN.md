# Brain-to-body integration work packages

This is the agreed ten-step sequence from the conversation. Implementing a work package and passing its scientific acceptance criteria are separate outcomes. Failed gates remain visible and block promoting an experimental controller as validated behavior.

1. Preserve the current version and establish a pinned model reference: verify ordering, signs, units, delays, annotations and equation responses.
2. Diagnose persistent activity with quiet, walking, odor, looming and recovery controls; identify adapter versus reference-model limitations.
3. Document exact sensory, intermediate and descending root IDs and sources within the complete network.
4. Independently verify the fixed movement decoder: stopping, forward drive and left/right turning. Retain explicit steering diagnostics.
5. Connect bilateral local odor measurements, document the encoding, and compare mirrored stimuli and sensory-off/replayed controls. Require downstream and behavioral responses.
6. Connect actual eye images. Verify eye orientation, moving-pattern, approaching-object and wall responses. Explicitly label engineered processing where biological retinal mappings are missing. Require downstream motor response and sensory-blanking controls.
7. Measure observation-to-neuron-to-movement timing and add contact/motion input only where ascending mappings are supported. Preserve continuous neural state, shared simulation time and deterministic playback-independent commands.
8. Establish causal behavior with matched input removal, pathway interruption and motor replay controls; at least 20 matched seeded main behavioral trials with prechosen metrics.
9. Progress through cue response, orientation, obstacle navigation, unfamiliar layouts, approaching threats and dangerous foraging, retaining failures and stall measurements.
10. Make the viewer explain representative sensory, intermediate and motor activity with absolute and baseline-relative firing. Revisit learning with independently controlled acquisition, retention and reversal evidence.

Results and limits are recorded in each stage's report. No stage earns navigation, learning, biological fidelity or consciousness claims from visualization alone.
