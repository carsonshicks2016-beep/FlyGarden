# Motor architecture evidence and decision boundary

Primary literature was checked on 2026-10-06. This is a prospective decision note, not a new controller or a promotion result.

The bilateral DNa02 activity difference is a defensible steering readout hypothesis. The recordings and perturbations in [Rayshubskiy et al., Neural circuit mechanisms for steering control](https://elifesciences.org/articles/102230) relate these neurons to turning and distinguish normal recruitment from bilateral perturbation effects. This supports testing a lateralized decoder; it does not guarantee that our synthetic unilateral ORN stimulation recruits the corresponding side. The current diagnostic instead finds predominantly left DNa02 activity for both odor sides.

[Braun et al., Descending networks transform command signals into population motor control](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07523-9) shows that DNp09 activation can recruit a broader descending network, including steering neurons. Consequently, walking support is not a neutral additive forward command. Its effects on other neurons must remain in matched support-only controls. The current support trials already show asymmetric DNa02 activity.

[Yang et al., Fine-grained descending control of steering](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12778575/) describes different stride effects and locomotor-phase relationships for DNa02 and DNg13. A scalar drive decoder with a supplied gait controller is therefore an engineered body interface. Adding DNg13 or summing every descending neuron does not, by itself, reproduce biological leg control or repair sensory choice.

Keep the existing decoder fixed through this matrix and its physical replay. First separate target stimulation, downstream propagation, bilateral specificity, state-support effects, and recovery. Use the archived Step4 inhibitory-intervention diagnosis as evidence; do not turn a lesion into a production repair. Any revised encoder, operating state, or decoder needs a separate version, biological justification, disjoint bounded calibration, and frozen held-out controls. No arbitrary neuron indices, threshold weakening, or hidden navigation.

The matrix's touch pulse and recorded eye fixtures are interface diagnostics. Live physical sensing, natural receptive fields, odor attraction, threat escape, and behavioral learning remain separate acceptance requirements.
