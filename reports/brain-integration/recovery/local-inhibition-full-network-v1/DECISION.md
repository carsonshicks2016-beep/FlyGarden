# Decision: retain experimental bridge; do not promote

The engineering integration passes: native delayed input arrivals, rate units, residual edge allocation, delayed continuous output, exact checkpoint continuation, unchanged weights through trials, observer parity, and independent input/decoder reconstruction.

The scientific diagnostic fails. All 12 candidate response/recovery checks and all four candidate contrast checks fail their frozen combined gates. The original arm also fails. In candidate recovery windows, the largest individual DM1 PN difference from same-model support-only is 224–236 Hz, against a 5-Hz limit. The first candidate left-cue trial has mean PN activity 225 Hz in the final half-second; its matched support-only trial is 0 Hz. No higher gain, altered sign list, changed criterion or new anatomical assignments were fitted to these outcomes.

The local mechanism is engaged rather than absent: in that recorded left-cue trial, 207 of 212 local nodes have nonzero activity, and left-DM1 local release proxies average about 99.6 Hz in the final recovery window. Their recorded incoming filtered drive averages 1.326 dimensionless units, beyond the engineered unit cap. Thus continuing local activity is consistent with continuing network input; the earlier isolated pulse-off check still passes. This descriptive evidence does not identify the causal feedback path, establish biological saturation or justify raising a gain.

Next work should identify which incoming pathways keep the mapped cells and PNs driven after cue offset, quantify local output versus remaining spiking input/output allocation, and review supported presynaptic/receptor feedback mechanisms. Freeze any subsequent candidate with separate calibration and prospective acceptance seeds. Keep this result as a failed mechanism screen, not a repaired brain, useful steering signal or learning result.

The original live application, learned flies, recordings and checkpoints remain unchanged. The experimental run checkpoints are separately retained. No body challenge or learning experiment was launched on this failed candidate.
