# Qualified patchy-neuron sign hypothesis bridge

The pinned FlyWire783 annotation table assigns exactly12 modeled roots the type lLN2P_b and known transmitter string `gaba, MIP; acetylcholine-negative`, citing Sizemore et al.2023 immunostaining and Croset et al.2018 single-cell RNA sequencing. All12 have top label gaba with low confidence and all12 have positive imported outgoing weights. Exact root IDs, row positions, source hashes and edge counts are frozen in protocol.json before trials. Negative acetylcholine staining is absence evidence, not a transmitter identity.

Sizemore et al.2023 identifies MIPergic patchy LNs that express GAD1 and lack ChAT/VGlut overlap. This supports inhibitory GABA identity for the population. The mapping to our exact roots is provided by the pinned annotation, not a transmitter measurement of the imaged individual. The paper does not by itself establish that every lLN2P_b cell is in its driver population. [Primary paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10465596/)

A2025 primary preprint explicitly relates R32F10 expression to approximately12 lLN2Ps, demonstrates GABA immunolabeling, and identifies nonspiking, spatially restricted responses. Its approximate driver count is not proof of one-to-one root correspondence. It is supporting cell-type evidence and is labeled a preprint, not peer-reviewed confirmation. Cached XML DOI10.1101/2025.08.19.671125 is hashed. [Preprint](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12393321/)

Peer-reviewed electrophysiology also shows that R32F10 patchy neurons can be nonspiking and spatially compartmentalized. That invalidates any claim that sign changes alone reproduce these cells. A limited sign-only sensitivity test remains useful for testing whether the imported positive-output assumption contributes to persistence. It cannot supply a validated graded-release or peptide model. [Primary electrophysiology](https://doi.org/10.1523/ENEURO.0109-22.2022)

Decision: evidence is adequate for an explicitly qualified, nonpromoted sign hypothesis. Use original, il3-only, patchy-only and combined identities to isolate group effects. Keep the same frozen response/recovery and contrast requirements as the preceding screen. Preserve failed outcomes and do not fit signs to successful behavior. No glutamate change, threshold tuning, receptor assumptions, invented kinetics, hidden navigation or application changes.
