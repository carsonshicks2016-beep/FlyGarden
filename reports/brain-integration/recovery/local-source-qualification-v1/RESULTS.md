## What physiology supports

Experimentally identified excitatory local-neuron populations can spike, reciprocally interact with PNs, and couple to PNs electrically. In the Yaksi and Wilson recordings, electrical coupling carries both depolarization and hyperpolarization; the same populations also recruit inhibitory neurons. This makes a uniform spike-to-positive-chemical-jump representation a substantive modeling assumption. It does not give an exact electrical edge map for these FlyWire roots or authorize converting their anatomical chemical edges to electrical connections. [Yaksi and Wilson, 2010](https://wilson.hms.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum8421/files/wilson-lab/files/yaksiwilson2010.pdf), [Huang et al., 2010 abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20869598/).

Marker identity, driver-labeled experimental population, exact reconstructed cell, spiking/graded physiology and fitted intrinsic parameters are separate claims. **Zero exact-root adaptation parameter fits are qualified here.** No reviewed source establishes a 450 ms decay or 13.5 mV adaptation increment for these roots. Missing mappings remain unavailable in results.json; they are not replaced by convenient assignments.

## Anatomical size and lateralization

Cached version-783 skeletons join exactly to left PN `720575940630770042` and right PN `720575940619071005`. Their full reconstructions have 16,810 and 16,922 vertices, and approximately 10,435 and 10,301 micrometers of cable. These whole-cell measures include axons; they do not establish dendritic surface area, capacitance, input resistance or synaptic attenuation. Cache hashes and those limitations are recorded in morphology-inspection.json.

Primary DM6 work found that differences in dendrite size/input resistance can compensate for unequal synapse number, and that ipsilateral synapse-number differences can explain lateralization without an additional per-site conductance multiplier. This motivates checking effective input scale in our uniform point-neuron model. It does not supply a fit for our exact DM1 roots. [Tobin et al., 2017](https://elifesciences.org/articles/24838).

DM1-specific unilateral stimulation and DM1/DM6 PN recordings support the relevance of olfactory side information. The often quoted roughly 40% release asymmetry is not an exact-root DM1 calibration and should not be added indiscriminately to our synaptic weights. [Gaudry et al., 2013](https://wilson.hms.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum8421/files/wilson-lab/files/gaudrywilson2013.pdf).

## Provenance and outcome

The frozen protocol pins the script, annotations, previous attribution/sign/domain artifacts and initial literature retrieval receipts. Tobin's full primary XML was cached and hashed. Yaksi/Wilson and Gaudry/Wilson full author PDFs were subsequently read through the browser after direct download failed; no local PDF cache is claimed. Huang was reviewed at abstract level. reviewed-primary-sources.json distinguishes those access levels without changing the pinned retrieval receipts.

The inventory took **0.44 seconds**. The evidence supports a clearly labeled local-only engineering mechanism test; it does not qualify a biological correction. Prior failed experiments and production source remain unchanged.
