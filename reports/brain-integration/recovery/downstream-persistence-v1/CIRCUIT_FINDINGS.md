# What the downstream tests establish

In two matched full-network states, positive modeled inputs from exactly annotated
ALLNs account for 97.85% and 97.91% of accepted positive delivery to the two DM1
projection neurons in the late tail. Cutting that group at odor offset silences
both PNs; cutting all positive PN inputs produces the same late PN result.

Removing the original odor afferents or positive output from those two PNs does
not silence the PNs. Removing positive connections within ALLNs lowers the class
mean from about131Hz to72Hz, but the PNs still fire at204/205Hz. Therefore the
ALLN-only recurrent group is not a complete explanation; outside inputs and
other coupled circuits remain candidates. Other projection neurons were not
removed by the DM1-output intervention.

Silencing the two DM1 PNs leaves left DNa02 firing at44/56Hz. This prevents a claim
that repairing those two sensory neurons alone will fix steering. The exploratory
accepted-delivery ranking after this intervention identifies PS013 root
720575940620842111 as the largest positive source in both seeds, with LAL030b,
LAL170 and other sources also active. This is a ranked hypothesis, not a causal
PS013 result. These sources have cell-type labels but missing cell-class labels;
no broader biological class or function has been invented for them.

Next bounded work should examine active inputs that sustain the residual ALLN
subset and test exact, preregistered steering-source interventions separately.
Do not graft a neuron-specific adaptation rule or change graph signs just because
it removes firing. A useful new controller still requires source-supported model
choices, recovery, bilateral direction, held-out body checks and later learning
validation. None of the current lesioned networks is a proposed application mode.

This evidence is conditional on the tested original model, engineered left-odor
encoding,65Hz supplied walking support and two seeds. It neither identifies a
unique minimal feedback loop nor validates a biological mechanism.
