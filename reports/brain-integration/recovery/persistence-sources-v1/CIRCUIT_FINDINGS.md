# Conditional circuit dependence, not a promoted repair

Fifteen four-second full-network diagnostics completed; all 2,400 recording chunks passed independent checks. Instrumentation-on/off trajectories matched exactly. Two diagnostic seeds are not navigation or learning acceptance.

## Local/projection circuit

In the background where positive ALLN-to-ALLN recurrence was already cut, the four selected local neurons still fired at 204 Hz. Annotated ALPN sources supplied 98.12% and 98.07% of their accepted positive modeled voltage increments in the final half second. These are summed increments, not biological membrane currents.

Adding an ALPN-to-ALLN positive-input cut silenced all four selected local neurons, the full ALLN and ALPN populations, and the selected DM1 projection neurons. Left steering activity fell to 0 and 2 Hz. This establishes dependence on that additional edge set in an already modified background. It does not show that cutting ALPN inputs alone would quiet the intact model, identify a unique minimal loop, or justify deleting connections in the application.

## Separate steering dependence

In a different background with positive ALLN-to-DM1-PN input cut, left DNa02 fired at 54 and 50 Hz. Removing its exact PS013 source input reduced this to 30 Hz in both seeds. Removing four selected source inputs reduced it to 8 and 6 Hz, but failed the frozen strong-suppression requirement of at most 10% of reference in both seeds. Cutting every positive input to left DNa02 produced 0 Hz; that is a sanity control, not a proposed controller.

The four sources were chosen from prior published top-12 lists, not a complete pooled all-source ranking. Their missing cell-class annotations remain unavailable. Anatomical labels alone do not establish function or biological sign.

## Next decision

Audit the imported signs and neuron parameters of the identified local/projection module against source-supported physiology before proposing a versioned dynamics revision. If necessary, a separately frozen ALPN-input-only comparison can distinguish the joint intervention from its components. Preserve the original model, fixed sensory encoding and motor decoder. Do not promote a lesioned graph, relax recovery thresholds, or substitute a hidden navigator. Navigation, behavioral learning, biological causation and the overall delivery goal remain unproven.
